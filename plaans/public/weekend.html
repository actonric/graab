SF Events · weekend plan

Three days, one toddler, several beer gardens

Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September. Everything below is on the SF Events calendar — 46 new entries went on this morning. This is the shortlist, in the order you'd actually do it.

Fri 63°/57° partly cloudy
Sat 63°/57° sunny
Sun 63°/57° sunny

Dry and mild the whole weekend, wind light at 4–5 mph — nothing here gets rained out. Two caveats: Axios SF called Thursday the start of a short warm spell, so the inland Peninsula options (Redwood City, Mountain View) may run genuinely hot rather than SF-cool; and Candlestick Point is exposed enough that "light wind" still means layers for Thomas.

Friday

Sept 11

A quiet afternoon and then a choice: stay local for Mozart on the Noe Valley plaza, or drive north for the last San Rafael night market of the year. San Rafael is the better evening.

Campfire Program — Presidio Tunnel Tops

Noe Valley Town Square, 3861 24th St · free

Play area on siteOutdoorsStroller excellent

One hour, outdoors, and the Town Square has a small play area, so Thomas can wander off mid-movement without it being a whole thing. Food and drink on site but not a proper bar.

This square is a three-for-one this weekend — farmers market with live music here Saturday morning, art festival here Sunday.

Beer and wine gardens, lion dancing, five blocks of closed street. The catch is the crush — Grant's sidewalks are narrow and it gets dense after about 6:30. Go at 5, leave by 6:30, and it works with a stroller.

Saturday

Sept 12

The busiest day by a distance, and it splits three ways: a Golden Gate Park morning, an Embarcadero morning, or the Bayview run that ends at the kite festival you already have tickets for. Pick one lane — they don't combine.

Lane A
10:30am on

★ Golden Gate Park: Singing in the Park, then the Whale's Tail

Mothers Meadow 10:30–11:30 · Whale's Tail 12–6 at 14th Ave East Meadow

Koret playground next doorBeer, wine, cocktailsStroller excellent

Group singing on a blanket in a meadow — children, adults and dogs all explicitly welcome, look for the pink blanket. Mothers Meadow is right beside the Koret Children's Quarter playground and the carousel, so the sing is thirty minutes and then Thomas gets the best play area in the city.

Then the Whale's Tail, which is the answer to "beer outdoors where a toddler is genuinely welcome": free entry, open meadow, lawn games, family- and dog-friendly, rotating live music. Saturday's lineup is Jon Bennett, Karaj Lost Coast, Kevin Coffey and Roman Antonian. The de Young is free to Bay Area residents that day if the fog wins.

Lane B
11am on

★ The Embarcadero stack

Ferry Building → Embarcadero Plaza → Levi's Plaza, all walkable

Drinks at two of threeFace paintingStroller perfect

Three things in a row on flat pavement. 11:00–11:45, Pim Techamuanvivit's free Thai cooking demo in the Foodwise tent at the Ferry Plaza market — recipes and samples for everyone, which is this weekend's "farmers market with something on". 11–6, the SF Turkish Festival at Embarcadero Plaza, 48 vendors, the best stroller surface of any fair this weekend. 11–4, Plaza Palooza at Levi's Plaza: face painting, games, Off the Grid trucks, drinks for sale, and Levi's Plaza has grass, a stream and shade.

Lane C
11am–4pm

★ The Bayview run, ending at your kite tickets

India Basin 11–2 · Candlestick Point 1–4

Two real kid programsWindy, bring layersStroller good

SF Transit Fair Family FunDay at India Basin Waterfront Park — learn-to-ride classes, button making, zine-making on the Muni Raised Me art bus, free food to the first 100 people, and a brand-new park with a genuine playground and new paved paths.

Then Kite by the Bay, which you're already booked for: kite decorating, learn-to-fish, activity tables from the Randall Museum and the Exploratorium, taco truck, DJ. The kite field is grass and dirt rather than path, and Candlestick is reliably windy.

Waller at Buchanan · free

Walk from homeOutdoors

This was on the calendar from Duboce Dads with a guessed 12–4 window; the real hours are 10:30–5:30, now corrected. Small, mellow, and the only one on the list you can walk to. Detail is thin because the organiser only posts to Facebook.

Richmond Autumn Moon Festival — petting zoo

Clement St, 8th to 11th Ave · free · already on the calendar

Petting zooStroller good, crowded

The strongest single toddler draw inside the city on Saturday. Petting zoo, giant mooncake cutting, 100+ vendors with hot food for the first time this year. Ten minutes from the Whale's Tail, so it pairs with Lane A if you're willing to move the car.

★ Makers Market — Mill Valley Lumber Yard

129 Miller Ave, Mill Valley · free

Beer and wine on siteSmall and shadedStroller excellent

The other genuine beer-plus-toddler answer, and the calmer one — fifteen makers in a small shaded courtyard with live music, not a crowd to push through. Milkweed Kids has a grand opening nearby 10:30–12:30.

11am–7pm

★ Mountain View Art and Wine Festival

Castro St, downtown Mountain View · free · runs again Sunday 10–6

8-vintner wine loungeDedicated kids zoneStroller excellent

The biggest street fair of the weekend by a wide margin — 400+ artists, several music stages, a wine tasting lounge with eight boutique vintners, and an actual kids zone. It's a drive, and the warm spell will be felt more down there.

Sunday

Sept 13

The easiest day to get right. Golden Gate Park does four free things inside a fifteen-minute walk from about 1pm, with a beer garden in the middle of them. Do a market in the morning first.

37th Ave between Ortega and Pacheco · free

The "Artship" runs free art activities and kids storytelling every single week — standing programming, not a one-off. Twenty-five vendors, live music, closed street. Still no word on the Cole Valley Nights fall date from these organisers.

Friendship bracelets, make-your-own art, plus activities run by the Eureka Learning Center, and the square's own play area for when he's done. Live music 11–4. Twenty-two artists. The nearest good thing to home on Sunday.

Spreckels Temple of Music, the Bandshell · free

Very watchableBench seatingStroller excellent

Ballet Folklorico Mexicano de Carlos Moreno dancing in costume, which is exactly the sort of thing that holds a fifteen-month-old. Benches plus open plaza to toddle.

1:30–3:30pm

Opera in the Park

Robin Williams Meadow · free, no ticket · already on the calendar

15,000 peopleNo glass, no tarpsStroller excellent

Six to ten minutes' walk from the Bandshell, so catch the first half of the Band and then move. First-come seating, so go early for a spot at the back where a wandering toddler is nobody's problem.

Rules worth knowing: blankets or low lawn chairs only, no tarps on the meadow grass, no glass. Four free water stations, food and drink for sale, free bike valet, parking very limited.

14th Ave East Meadow · Bandshell · both free

Beer, wine, cocktailsAll agesStroller excellent

11am and 2pm

★ The Peninsula alternative: Redwood City

Magical Bridge Playground 11am · Courthouse Square 2–8pm · both free

Built for his ageKids areaStroller excellent

If you'd rather drive than do the park: Andy Z's Magical Music and Motion is the single best pure-toddler event of the weekend — a proper children's performer, interactive, staged at a fully accessible inclusive playground, so the concert and the playground are the same place and there's no leaving moment.

Then Fiestas Patrias at Courthouse Square from 2pm: dedicated kids area, folklorico, mariachi, Mexican food, Grito de Independencia at 6. Ten minutes apart. No beer confirmed at either, which is the trade-off against Golden Gate Park.

Two things worth knowing that aren't events.

A mountain lion was sighted in the Panhandle near Fell and Baker around 6 September and was still at large as of the last update in Duboce Families, from a KCBS journalist in the group. Relevant if any of the Golden Gate Park plan involves the east end early or late.

Garfield Square rec centre in the Mission opens its multipurpose room for tot time, 10am–noon on Tuesdays and possibly Thursdays with bilingual story time. Came up in Parents in the Parklet. Not this weekend, but it's a standing thing and it'll go on the calendar once the cadence is confirmed.
