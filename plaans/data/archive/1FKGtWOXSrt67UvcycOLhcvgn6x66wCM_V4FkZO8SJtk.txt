SECTION 6 — TOOL GOTCHAS  (bit me this session)
=====================================================================
list_events caps at 250 results and SILENTLY TRUNCATES THE DATE WINDOW.
  Always check nextPageToken + the last event's date, or you'll report false deletions.
update_event CANNOT change a recurrence rule. Delete and recreate instead.
Calendar attachments must be Google DRIVE files; raw image URLs are rejected.
create_event accepts EXDATE;TZID=… alongside RRULE in recurrenceData (for skipped weeks).
Description text is NOT a reliable marker — ChatGPT also edits this calendar and has
  overwritten "Added by Claude" lines. Track by EVENT ID only.
