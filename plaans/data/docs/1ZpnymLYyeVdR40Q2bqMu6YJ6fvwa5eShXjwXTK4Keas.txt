*** THE HAYES VALLEY CLUSTER — the highest-yield discovery of this run ***


hayespromenade.org/events
  A [MINED] — SINGLE RICHEST SOURCE FOUND THIS RUN. The merchant/promenade organisation for
  the Hayes St corridor. Walkable from Duboce. Carries everything the Hayes Valley
  Neighborhood Association's own calendar has PLUS four October events and a November event
  HVNA omits entirely, PLUS the Hayes Valley ENTERTAINMENT ZONE — an officially designated
  zone, 4-10pm daily through 2026-11-28, in which TO-GO ALCOHOLIC DRINKS ARE LEGAL ON THE
  STREET. That last item is the star test met exactly (beer outdoors, open street, stroller,
  daylight) and no other source on this list carries it.
  Yielded 5 of this run's 24 adds. TREAT AS TIER A.
  LESSON WORTH GENERALISING: for a neighbourhood, check BOTH the residents' association AND
  the merchants'/promenade organisation. They publish different things and neither is a
  superset of the other.


hayesvalleysf.org/calendar/ and /events/
  A [MINED]. Hayes Valley Neighborhood Association. Real forward calendar, strong Sept-Oct
  2026, nothing past October. Carries the PROXY outdoor film series and the Fleet Week
  neighbourhood brass band concert at Patricia's Green. Does NOT carry the Entertainment
  Zone. Read alongside hayespromenade.org, never instead of it.
  ADDRESS CONFLICT RECORDED, NOT RESOLVED: HVNA gives the Hayes Valley Farmers' Market
  address as 450 Hayes St; Hayes Promenade gives 485 Hayes St. Both are organiser-side, so
  the "organiser wins" rule cannot adjudicate. They are half a block apart at Patricia's
  Green. Immaterial in practice; recorded so nobody re-investigates.

  Heights"; the Randall Museum sits inside the park and runs its programming. Free, all ages,

  RECOMMENDATION: check monthly, not quarterly. A quarterly cadence will miss everything.
