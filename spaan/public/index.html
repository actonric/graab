spaan

multi-year family finances

Summary

Settings

Amounts are annual, in start-year money, and rise with inflation unless a line says otherwise.

People

Use a person's name plus an age to time a line, like thomas@5.

Balance over time

Year by year

Income

After-tax, per year. Leave From or To empty for open-ended. A negative income models time off or going part-time.

Expenses

Per year. Tie childcare and school to a child's age so they start and stop on their own.

One-offs

A single year's cost: a deposit, a car, a big trip. Negative for money coming in.
